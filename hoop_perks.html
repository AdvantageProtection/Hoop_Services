Free Mortgage Advice for Hoop Staff

Whether you are buying your first home or your fifth, the advice and the broker fee are on us.

Through our partnership with Advantage Financial Solutions, you have access to a team of 15 specialist mortgage advisers who work with the whole of the market. Whether you are employed, self-employed or have a more complex income, they will find the right lender and position your application properly.

Staff cost Mortgage advice is completely free, with no broker fee.

How the mortgage process works

  1. Initial free consultationA quick, informal chat with a specialist adviser to review your circumstances and homebuying goals.
  2. Borrowing assessment & market researchThe team assesses your income across the market to find your maximum borrowing and the lenders offering the best rates for you.
  3. Agreement in principle (AIP)Your adviser secures an AIP so you can make offers on properties with confidence.
  4. Full application & lifetime supportYour adviser handles the application end to end, liaising with underwriters, estate agents and solicitors through to completion, with no broker fee for you.

How the advisory process works

  1. Initial 20-minute callA brief, informal discussion to understand your situation, with an overview of Income Protection, Life Insurance and Critical Illness Cover. Details of existing policies are useful but not essential.
  2. Tailored market researchLaurence researches the market for the most comprehensive and cost-effective cover based on your health, earnings and lifestyle.
  3. Options & recommendations summaryYou receive a clear summary of the options and the factors that affect cost, then Laurence tailors policies to your budget and requirements.
  4. Application & underwriting supportLaurence manages the application, handles the paperwork and liaises directly with underwriters so your cover is set up accurately.
