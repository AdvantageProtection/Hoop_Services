Specialist, tax-efficient cover

We look after a team of 15 mortgage advisers and their clients' personal and business protection needs. The two policies driving the strongest introducer partnerships are Relevant Life Cover and Executive Income Protection — once niche products, now offered by every mainstream provider and extremely popular with company directors.

Relevant Life Cover

Company-funded life insurance for directors. Premiums are paid by the business as an allowable business expense — saving on Corporation Tax — without incurring a Benefit-in-Kind charge. Personal cover, at a fraction of the personal cost.

Executive Income Protection

Income protection funded through the company, providing a tax-efficient replacement income for a director if illness or injury stops them working. A natural extension of the tax planning you already deliver.

How the referral model works

  1. You make the introduction Flag any client who could benefit from tax-efficient protection and pass their details to us — by form, email, or a three-way introduction. That's the entire commitment on your side.
  2. We advise, free of charge Laurence, our specialist adviser, provides your client with completely free, independent advice — researching the whole market and recommending the most suitable, tax-efficient structure.
  3. We handle everything else Applications, underwriting, and compliance are managed by us from start to finish. Your client is looked after professionally and kept informed at every stage.
  4. You receive the commission share Once the policy is set up, your practice receives its compliant share of the commission — typically £250 to £2,000+ per client, paid promptly and fully documented.
