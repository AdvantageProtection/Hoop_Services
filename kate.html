Protection Advice for Locums & Contractors

100% free · whole-of-market · no obligation

Independent working means no employer-funded sick pay and no death-in-service benefits. This specialist advisory service helps you explore how to protect yourself and your family financially — so an illness or injury doesn't have to become a financial crisis.

The service is completely free from start to finish. If you decide to put a policy in place, Advantage FS is paid commission by the insurer — never by you. There's no pressure and no obligation; the goal is simply to make sure you have all the facts and clear options.

What that covers

  • Maintaining a reliable income — Income Protection provides a monthly income if an injury or illness stops you from working.
  • Keeping your home and mortgage covered — Life Insurance ensures your family could stay in their home if the unexpected happens.
  • Lump-sum financial security — Critical Illness Cover pays out a lump sum if you're diagnosed with a serious condition.
  • Ongoing support for your family — Family Income Benefit provides regular financial support over time rather than a single payment.
  • Protecting your property — Home Insurance, whether buildings, contents, or combined cover.
How it works: a friendly 20–30 minute call to understand your setup and what you want to protect. You'll then receive a comprehensive written summary of all your options, costs and policy features — so you can review everything in your own time, with zero obligation.

Mortgage Advice for Locums & Contractors

100% free advice · access to 100+ lenders

Being self-employed or working on a contract basis can make securing property financing feel complicated through high-street lenders. This specialist mortgage service helps you navigate the market — including understanding how lenders assess variable or non-traditional earnings.

Whether you're buying your first home, moving up the property ladder, or looking at a remortgage, the team will explore your options with you — and with access to 100+ lenders, they dig deeper to find the right deal for your circumstances.

What they help with

First-time buyers Home movers Remortgages Buy-to-let investments Variable & non-traditional earnings Adverse credit history Later life mortgages (over 50s)

What you get

  • Free initial consultation — a zero-obligation chat about your goals and what you could borrow.
  • No Offer, No Fee — you only pay once a formal mortgage offer has been secured.
