🛡️ Protection & Insurance

Protecting your most valuable asset — you

Your ability to earn is your most valuable asset, and we help you protect it. Everything starts with a friendly, free review, after which you'll receive a comprehensive summary of your options, costs and policy features — with absolutely no obligation to take anything out.

  • Life Insurance — financial security for your loved ones if the worst happens.
  • Income Protection — covers your income if illness or injury stops you working.
  • Critical Illness Cover — a lump sum payment if you're diagnosed with a serious condition.
  • Family Income Benefit — ongoing financial support for your family.
  • Home Insurance — buildings, contents, or combined cover to protect your property.
