Before your call

A little preparation helps Laurence give you the most accurate picture of your options during the 20-minute call. Nothing here is essential — but having details to hand makes the conversation more productive.

  • Existing policies If you hold any protection policies from previous employment or past advice, having the provider names and cover amounts handy is useful (but not essential).
  • Rough monthly earnings A ballpark figure of your typical monthly income helps Laurence size any Income Protection recommendation correctly.
  • Monthly commitments Your mortgage or rent, and any other major outgoings, help determine the level of cover that would keep your household afloat.
  • A quiet 20 minutes The call is informal, but a quiet space with a cup of tea means you can focus and ask anything on your mind.
