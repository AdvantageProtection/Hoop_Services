Why income protection matters

For locums, your earnings depend entirely on the shifts you work — losing your health means losing your income overnight.

  • Replaces lost earnings — regular tax-free monthly income if illness or injury stops you working.
  • Covers commitments — mortgage/rent, bills and living costs stay paid.
  • Tailored to you — structured around your earnings, waiting periods and budget.
  • Bridges the gap — your own private sick pay scheme while you recover.
