Income Protection: A Key Focus for Self-Employed Professionals

For most locums and contractors, Income Protection is the most valuable safety net you can have. Because your earnings depend entirely on the shifts you work, losing your health means losing your income overnight. Here is why income protection is such a powerful tool for self-employed professionals:

  • Replaces Your Lost Earnings: if an accident or illness stops you from working shifts, the policy pays out a regular, tax-free monthly income to keep your household afloat.
  • Covers All Your Financial Commitments: it ensures that your mortgage or rent, utility bills, food, and daily living costs are taken care of without dipping into your savings.
  • Tailored to How You Work: policies can be structured around your specific earnings, waiting periods, and budget, meaning you only pay for the exact cover level you need.
  • Bridges the Workplace Gap: since you don't receive employer sick pay, this steps in as your own private sick pay scheme, giving you complete peace of mind while you focus entirely on recovery.

Laurence will give a comprehensive overview of all the options available for Income Protection, Life Insurance & Critical Illness Cover. Why Specialist Mortgage Advice Matters for Locums

For self-employed professionals and contractors, securing a mortgage isn't just about how much you earn — it is about how your income is assessed:

  • Day Rate & Contract Assessment: specialist lenders can base borrowing power on your daily contract rate rather than relying strictly on two or three years of submitted accounts.
  • Navigating Income Variations: advisers know which lenders are comfortable with gaps between locum contracts, short contract histories, or flexible working hours.
  • Cutting Through Red Tape: by placing your application with lenders who understand your employment model, you avoid unnecessary delays, automatic declines, or lower borrowing limits from automated high-street scoring.
  • Whole-of-Market Access: accessing specialist lenders across the market ensures you get competitive interest rates tailored to your exact borrowing profile.

How the Mortgage Process Works

  1. Initial Free Consultation: a quick, informal discussion with a specialist mortgage adviser to review your career setup, contract structure, and homebuying goals.
  2. Borrowing Assessment & Market Research: the team analyses your specific income setup across the market to determine maximum borrowing capacity and locate lenders offering the best rates for your scenario.
  3. Agreement in Principle (AIP): once the right lender and strategy are identified, your adviser secures an Agreement in Principle, allowing you to make offers on properties with confidence.
  4. Full Application & Lifetime Support: your dedicated adviser handles the end-to-end application process — liaising directly with underwriters, estate agents, and solicitors to take your mortgage smoothly through to completion.
